Three calls I made that you may want to overrule:

- **I didn't rank the two named causes.** 3 versus 2 out of 8 isn't a real ordering — that gap is noise at this sample size. The existing "often… also" already implied env-var-first and the data is consistent with it, so I left the order alone rather than promoting it to "most common cause," which the numbers don't support.
- **The load-bearing number is the 3, not the 3.** The entry currently reads as a complete list; the data says it covers 5 of 8. That's the change worth making regardless of how the other five split, so I put the emphasis there.
- **"Last quarter" needs pinning before you commit it.** It stops being true in three months, and this document is otherwise careful to date its claims ("the eleven weeks since deploy"). Substitute the actual range.

The open question: what were the other three? If they share a cause, it's a named cause covering more incidents than the migration one does, and it belongs in the runbook above the counts — at which point the counts themselves probably come back out, having done their job. If they were three unrelated one-offs, the entry above is the right permanent shape. Do you have the incident IDs?
