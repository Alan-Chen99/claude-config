# Bathroom renovation — status

**Done.** Demolition is finished: the old tiles and the old shower are out. It took two days instead of one because the floor tiles were glued straight to the concrete. The new drain and water lines are in and have been pressure tested. The floor tiles are laid but not grouted.

**Waiting on.** The shower glass, ordered three weeks ago, is promised for next Tuesday. The tiler cannot grout until the glass has been measured in place, so the floor sits ungrouted until then.

**Decision I made without you.** The electrician found the old ceiling light on the same circuit as the kitchen. I had a separate circuit added, for 400, which was not in the budget. The alternative was keeping the shared circuit, and that would have failed the inspection.

**Money.** 4700 spent of 6000. The remaining known costs are the grout and the labour for it, about 300, and the glass fitting, about 500. That leaves about 500 over if nothing else comes up.

**Your call, by Monday.** The tiler offers to redo the small wall behind the sink in the same tile for 350 while he is here. The old tile there is fine, it just does not match. I will not be reachable for a few days, so decide it without me.

**Risk.** If the glass is late again, the tiler moves on to another job and cannot come back for two weeks.
