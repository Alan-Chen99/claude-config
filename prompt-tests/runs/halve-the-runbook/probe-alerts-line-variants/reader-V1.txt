## Bottom line

There is nothing to turn up. `WORKERS=4` is already the ceiling: the vendor pool is 5 per tenant and the admin console holds 1, so 4 is the whole budget. Setting it to 5 takes the console's slot and produces the exact 503s you're trying to escape. `BATCH_SIZE=200` is the vendor's limit, not ours. `STRICT_ORDERING` is already 0. The throughput ceiling here is the vendor's pool, not your config.

That also changes the diagnosis. "503s are nearly always pool exhaustion; check `WORKERS` first" — so check it, but if it comes back 4 with one console session, you are inside budget and *your* pool is not the exhausted one. This incident is in the "nearly always" exception, and no knob on your side addresses it.

## The one knob that is actually turnable

`RETRY_BACKOFF=2s`, and you turn it **up**, not down. It has never been measured, so this is a guess — but it is a guess in the safe direction. Every 503 produces a retry, and at 2s those retries re-enter the same 4 slots and compete with first-attempt sends. That is a feedback loop: the more the vendor rejects, the less of your pool is doing new work, and the queue drains slower. Raising the backoff (with jitter, if the sender supports it) gives slots back to fresh sends. Config is read at boot, so this needs a restart; `SHUTDOWN_GRACE=30` drains in-flight sends first.

## Check rather than turn

1. **Is the synthetic check also failing?** It runs every minute against a fixed record id and alerts on its own when the vendor is down. If it is red, the vendor is degraded independent of your load — confirmation that this is them, and that tuning is pointless until it recovers.
2. **Is anything else eating pool slots?** More than one admin console session, or a second consumer, puts you over 5 without `WORKERS` changing. Confirm the running value is really 4 — a boot-time change to 5 would be invisible until you look.
3. **Is anyone looping `./redrive.sh`?** It consumes pool slots *and* risks the tenant rate-limit. February: ~900 records, rate-limited for an hour, threshold still unknown.

## If you genuinely need a 5th worker

Close the admin console and `WORKERS=5` fits the pool. I'd treat that as a deliberate, coordinated change rather than a load response: it works only as long as nobody opens the console, and whoever does will cause pool exhaustion with no obvious cause. If you take it, say so somewhere the next person will look.

## Don't

- **Don't loop `./redrive.sh`** to drain the backlog. An hour of tenant rate-limiting costs more than the backlog does.
- **Don't set `LOG_LEVEL=debug`** to investigate. It drops lines above ~2k/s, and under load you are above that — you'd lose exactly the lines you turned it on for.
- **Hold off on individual redrives** for stuck `pending` until the pool recovers; they compete for the same slots. `pending` over an hour is a lost acknowledgement, not a lost record, so those can wait.

The real fix is a conversation with the vendor about pool size per tenant. That is the binding constraint, and it isn't in your config.
