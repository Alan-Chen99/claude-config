# Archive — audit reports only

This directory is an archive of finished audit reports on ralph loop 3 and the
evidence artifacts they were written from. It is a record of what was written,
not a source of truth about the subject.

## Rules

- **Do not cite anything here.** Not in a report, not in a note, not in an
  answer to the user. A claim needed elsewhere must be re-derived from primary
  sources — the session logs, the repo, the run itself.
- **If you are producing another report, read nothing here** — no report, no
  `evidence/` artifact, not this file's neighbours. A fresh audit that has read
  the previous one is no longer independent: it inherits the earlier framing,
  its omissions, and its errors, and agreement between the two then means
  nothing.
- Reading is permitted only when the archive itself is the subject — comparing
  two reports, auditing how a past audit was conducted, or editing a file that
  lives here.
