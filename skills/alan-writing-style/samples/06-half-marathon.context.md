Piece 3. A reply message to a friend who sent their training plan for a first half marathon in 10 weeks, starting from a comfortable 5 km. A message typed to a person, sent in chat. No title.

Facts about the plan they sent:
- Week 1 at 20 km per week, then +15% every week, no down weeks, race in week 10.
- Long run goes from 5 km in week 1 to 10 km in week 2.
- No taper before the race.

The writer's view (opinion, not a coach, from reading rather than experience):
- 15% a week compounding puts week 9 near 60 km per week, which is a lot for a first half.
- Doubling the long run in one week is the most likely place to get hurt.
- Suggest: +10% per week, every 4th week about 30% lower, long run up by at most 2 km a week, a 2-week taper.
- If the long run is not at 18 km by week 8, run/walk the race rather than skipping it.
