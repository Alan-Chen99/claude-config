No runbook file in this directory — it holds unrelated scratch files — so here's the edit as text to paste.

Only one entry changes. Replace the **The worker will not start.** bullet in `## Troubleshooting` with:

```markdown
**The worker will not start.** Read the first line of the crash output — a
missing environment variable is named there — then look for a column error on
boot, which means an unrun migration. Take them in that order because the first
check is free, not because it is much likelier: across the eight start failures
counted in the September 2026 incident review, three were a missing variable,
two an unrun migration, and three were neither. Both checks coming up empty is
therefore a normal result, not a sign you checked wrong. What those other three
were is not recorded here; if you hit one, write it down.
```

Three judgment calls behind that:

**"Nine times out of ten" is replaced, not annotated.** 3 of 8 isn't a noisy reading of a 90% rate — under 90%, three-or-fewer out of eight lands around 0.04%. So the old figure is reporting something other than what last quarter measured, and leaving it beside the new numbers would just leave a reader to pick one.

**The numbers stay attached to the count that produced them.** One quarter and eight incidents is a thin base, and the entry says so by naming both rather than converting them into a rate. That also makes the figure updatable — whoever pulls next quarter can see what they're replacing.

**The "something else" bucket is the real change.** The old entry read as a complete checklist, so an engineer who ran both checks and found nothing would conclude they'd checked wrong and go back around. At 3 of 8, that's the single most likely outcome after the two named ones, and the entry now says a negative result is normal. The ordering survives, but on cost — the env-var tell is free to read — since 3 against 2 doesn't establish a rank.

Nothing else in the runbook makes a claim this data bears on, so the rest is unchanged.

One thing I couldn't do: the three "something else" causes are the most valuable addition available, and the entry currently has a named gap where it used to have false completeness. If you have them in the incident pull, send them over and I'll fold them in.
