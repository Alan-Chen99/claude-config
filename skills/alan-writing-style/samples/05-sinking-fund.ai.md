## Irregular expenses

Annual and semiannual bills hit the checking account unevenly: car insurance 1200 in March, home insurance 900 in September, car service about 600 twice a year, and gifts about 800 across the year. Last year this caused two overdrafts, both in months with an insurance bill.

**How it works.** These bills are funded from a separate savings account. On the 1st of each month, an automatic transfer moves one twelfth of the sum of the listed items. The list currently comes to 4100 per year, so the transfer is 342 per month. Bills on the list are paid from that account.

**The list.** A shared spreadsheet with columns for item, amount, month, and last actual. Anyone in the household can add an item; the transfer amount is recomputed on the next 1st.

**Overruns.** If an actual bill exceeds its estimate by more than 10%, the estimate is set to the actual. The shortfall that month is covered from checking — the transfer is not raised mid-year.

**History.** Year one had no margin above the sum, and the account ran dry in November when two items came in above estimate. Year two adds a 5% margin on top of the sum.

**Not covered.** One-off purchases, such as a new fridge. Those are a separate decision, not a list item.
