Yes — three, roughly in order of how directly they answer it.

**The synthetic check.** This is the closest thing you have to a vendor health probe. It runs every minute against a fixed record id, *independently of what you send*, so it exercises the vendor's ingest endpoint even when your own traffic is zero. If it's passing right now, the endpoint is answering. The runbook only mentions it as the explanation for alerts firing with no traffic, so you may have to go find where its results actually land — but it's the one continuous, traffic-independent probe you own. Caveat: fixed record id. It tells you the endpoint responds, not that real settlement records ingest correctly.

**The `pending` → `sent` transition.** A record goes `sent` only on the vendor's acknowledgement, so acks arriving means the vendor is processing. A fleet-wide stall at `pending` is a genuine vendor-side signal. Note the runbook's one-hour rule — "over an hour means a lost acknowledgement, not a lost record" — is written about *individual* records. One stuck record is ack loss; everything stuck at once is a different animal, and the rule doesn't cover it.

**A single redrive.** `./redrive.sh <id>` on one id is safe and is a deliberate probe of the real path — hand-off plus ack. But do not turn it into a polling loop to "keep checking": the February incident redrove ~900 records and rate-limited the tenant for an hour, and nobody knows the threshold. A repeated check is exactly the shape that trips it.

**Don't read 503s as vendor-down.** Nearly always pool exhaustion on your side. The vendor allows 5 connections per tenant, the admin console holds 1, and `WORKERS=4` consumes the remaining 4 exactly — no headroom. Any extra consumer produces 503s that look like the vendor and aren't. Check `WORKERS` first.

One practical aside: the vendor's admin console is a second vendor-side surface you can try when the status page is down, but reaching for it isn't free — it draws from the same 5-connection pool, so a second person opening it while all 4 workers are running will manufacture the 503s you're trying to diagnose.
