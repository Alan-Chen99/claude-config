Thanks for sending the plan over! Caveat up front: I'm not a coach, and this is from reading rather than from having done it. But a couple of things jumped out.

The main one is the ramp. Starting at 20 km in week 1 and adding 15% every week with no down weeks compounds harder than it looks — by week 9 you're near 60 km a week, which is a lot for a first half.

The other is the long run going from 5 km in week 1 to 10 km in week 2. Doubling it in one week is, I think, the most likely place in the whole plan to get hurt.

And there's no taper before the race, which seems like a miss with the race in week 10.

What I'd suggest instead: +10% per week instead of 15%, and every 4th week about 30% lower. Bring the long run up by at most 2 km a week. Then a 2-week taper into the race.

One more thing, since 10 weeks isn't much runway: if the long run isn't at 18 km by week 8, run/walk the race rather than skipping it. Finishing it that way still counts.
