- `WORKERS=4` — the vendor pool is 5 per tenant and the admin console holds 1. Four *is* the max. It's annotated "a ceiling, not a knob."

- `STRICT_ORDERING=0` — already off. Nothing to gain, and don't reason further from it: the belief that they ingest in send order comes from acks alone, never confirmed with them.

The runbook's "503s nearly always pool exhaustion; check `WORKERS` first" is asking you to check whether it's *too high*, not whether you can raise it. So:

**1. Verify `WORKERS` is actually 4.** If a past incident left it at 6 or 8, that's your 503 cause and turning it back down to 4 is the fix. Raising it above 4 makes the 503s worse and can lock the admin console out of its connection.

**2. Use the synthetic check as a free diagnostic.** It hits a fixed record id once a minute and alerts independently of your traffic. If it's *also* failing, the vendor is down more broadly and none of your config matters. If it's green while your sends 503, the exhaustion is your pool.

**3. The one knob with room is `RETRY_BACKOFF=2s` — and the move is up, not down.** It's explicitly never been measured, and flagged as the place to start when retries need work; retries need work now. Against *sustained* 503s, a 2s retry burns your four slots re-hitting a vendor that isn't ready, at the expense of fresh records. Try 4s and measure the drain rate. This won't make a healthy vendor faster — it stops you wasting capacity against an unhealthy one.

**4. Do not bulk-redrive to drain the backlog.** Never loop `./redrive.sh`. February: ~900 records, tenant rate-limited for an hour — throughput to zero. The threshold is unknown, so there's no safe batch size to discover under load.

**5. Some of that backlog may not be backlog.** `pending` past an hour is a lost acknowledgement, not a lost record. Check what fraction of the queue is >1h old before treating depth as undelivered volume.

Mechanics for the backoff change: config is read at boot, so it needs a restart. `SHUTDOWN_GRACE=30` drains in-flight sends and `rollout pause` is safe.
