Nothing in this config is safe to turn up — and turning up the obvious one will make it worse.

**`WORKERS` is not a throughput knob, it's already pinned to the ceiling.** The vendor allows 5 concurrent connections per tenant. The admin console permanently holds 1. That leaves exactly 4 for the relay — which is why `WORKERS=4`. There's no headroom: 4 + 1 = 5 = the vendor's hard limit. Raising `WORKERS` pushes total concurrency past what the vendor allows, which is the definition of pool exhaustion — you'd cause *more* 503s, not fewer.

That's also what "check `WORKERS` first" in the troubleshooting section actually means: check, not raise. It's telling you to verify nothing has drifted — that `WORKERS` is still 4 and the admin console isn't quietly holding more than its usual 1 connection. If either has crept up, that's your bug, and the fix is bringing it back down to the 4/1 split, not up.

Everything else in the file is a non-starter too:
- **`BATCH_SIZE`** — also vendor-imposed. Don't bump it without checking their docs; overshooting their limit is a likely new source of errors, not a throughput gain.
- **`RETRY_BACKOFF`** — free to change, but lowering it right now adds more retry traffic into an already-saturated pool, compounding the 503s. If you touch it at all during this incident, raise it, don't lower it — and that reduces retry pressure, it doesn't grow throughput.
- **`STRICT_ORDERING`, `LOG_LEVEL`, `SHUTDOWN_GRACE`** — unrelated to send concurrency or throughput.

So: first confirm `WORKERS` is actually still 4 and the admin console isn't over-holding — that's the one legitimate lead the runbook points you at. If both check out, you're not looking at a misconfiguration, you're at the vendor's real ceiling, and no env var fixes that. Your options are (a) get the vendor to raise the 5-per-tenant limit, or (b) shed/throttle load upstream and let the queue absorb it, using `redrive.sh <record-id>` afterward for anything that lands in `pending`. There's no config change that legitimately buys you more throughput here.
