## Irregular expenses

- Problem: annual and semiannual bills (car insurance 1200 in March, home insurance 900 in September, car service about 600 twice a year, gifts about 800 across the year) hit the checking account unevenly. Two overdrafts last year, both in months with an insurance bill.
- Mechanism: a separate savings account. On the 1st of each month an automatic transfer moves one twelfth of the sum of the listed items (currently 4100 per year, so 342 per month). Bills on the list are paid from that account.
- The list: a shared spreadsheet with columns item, amount, month, last actual. Anyone in the household can add an item; the transfer amount is recomputed on the next 1st.
- Overrun rule: if an actual bill exceeds its estimate by more than 10%, the estimate is set to the actual. The shortfall that month is covered from checking; the transfer is not raised mid-year.
- History: year one had no margin above the sum and the account ran dry in November (two items came in above estimate). Year two adds a 5% margin on top of the sum.
- Not covered: one-off purchases (a new fridge). Those are a separate decision, not a list item.
