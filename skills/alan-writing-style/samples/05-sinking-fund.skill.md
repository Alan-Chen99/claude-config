## Irregular expenses

Bills that arrive once or twice a year are paid from a separate savings account rather than from checking. On the 1st of each month an automatic transfer moves one twelfth of the sum of the items on "the list" -- currently 4100 per year, so 342 per month.

Reason: those bills land unevenly across the year.

- Car insurance. 1200 in March.
- Home insurance. 900 in September.
- Car service. About 600, twice a year.
- Gifts. About 800 across the year.

Two overdrafts last year, both in months with an insurance bill.

The list: a shared spreadsheet, columns

> item, amount, month, last actual

Anyone in the household can add an item, and the transfer amount is recomputed on the next 1st.

Overrun: a bill more than 10% above its estimate sets the estimate to the actual. The shortfall that month is covered from checking -- the transfer is not raised _mid-year_.

Margin: year one carried no margin above the sum, and the account ran dry in November with two items above estimate. Year two adds 5% on top of the sum.

Not covered: one-off purchases (example: a new fridge). Those are a separate decision rather than a list item.
