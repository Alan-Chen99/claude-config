**The blocking unknown: is `WORKERS` capped by the vendor or by you?**

The runbook says 503s are "nearly always pool exhaustion, check `WORKERS` first." That's ambiguous in a way that matters a lot at 2x volume:

- If pool exhaustion means the vendor's rate limit/concurrency cap, doubling `WORKERS` to handle doubled volume will *increase* 503s, not prevent them — you'd be hitting their ceiling twice as often.

Get the vendor's documented concurrency/rate limit before touching this value. That number gates almost everything else.
