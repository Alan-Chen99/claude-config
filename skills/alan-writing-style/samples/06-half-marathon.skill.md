the ramp is the problem: 20 km in week 1, +15% every week, no down weeks. that _compounds_ -- week 9 lands near 60 km a week. thats a lot for a first half (im not a coach, this is from reading rather than experience).

long run: 5 km in week 1, 10 km in week 2. doubling it in one week is the most likely place in the plan to get hurt.

taper: none, with the race in week 10.

what id change: +10% a week, every 4th week about 30% lower, long run up by at most 2 km a week, and a 2-week taper into the race.

if the long run isnt at 18 km by week 8, run/walk the race rather than skipping it.
