`WORKERS=4`.

That's the documented value, and it's the one to carry into the config. But copy it knowing what it is: the runbook says it "worked before; not tuned or engineered." It's a value that hasn't caused a problem, not a value anyone derived. Don't cite it downstream as a tuned setting, and don't treat 4 as a ceiling or a floor.

Two things from the runbook that bear on it later, not now:

- **503s point here first** — "nearly always pool exhaustion; check `WORKERS` first." The runbook doesn't say which direction to move it, so if you hit that, you're diagnosing, not applying a known fix.
- **Changing it leans on an unverified assumption.** `STRICT_ORDERING=0` rests on "we think they ingest in send order, from acks alone; never asked, and never documented by them." More workers means more concurrent sends riding on that belief. And with no staging (deliberate — finance turned down a second tenant in March), there's nowhere to test a different value except production.

So: 4 in the config now, and treat any future change to it as its own decision with the vendor question answered first.
